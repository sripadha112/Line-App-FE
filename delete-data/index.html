How to Withdraw Consent or Delete Your Data

Last Updated: August 10, 2026

If you want to withdraw consent or request deletion of your data, follow the steps below in the kedulz application.

Important: You can manage consent and request data deletion from inside your account profile.

Steps to Withdraw Consent or Delete Data

  1. Log in to the application.
  2. Go to your profile and scroll down to the bottom.
  3. You will see a Withdraw Consent option. Click it to open the next page.
  4. On that page, you will see all consents you gave during registration.
  5. You can withdraw any or all of your consents. We will process the request and clear your data as requested within 72 hours.
  6. You can also delete your medical data from that page. Medical data deletion requests are processed immediately.

Note: Once you withdraw consent or request data deletion, we will process and remove the requested data from our databases according to your request. If all required data is removed and you want to use our application again later, you may need to register again as a new user.
